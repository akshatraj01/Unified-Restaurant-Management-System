# Project Overview

## Title
Unified Restaurant Management System (URMS)

## Problem statement
Restaurant activities such as menu maintenance, customer registration, order processing, billing, inventory monitoring, staff records, and reservations can become difficult to coordinate when managed separately.

## Proposed solution
URMS is a modular C application that provides a centralized, file-based demonstration of common restaurant workflows.

## Objectives
- Maintain food and customer records.
- Process orders containing multiple food items.
- Calculate subtotal, discount, illustrative tax, and final total.
- Track kitchen/order status.
- Monitor ingredient quantities and low-stock thresholds.
- Maintain employee and reservation records.
- Generate basic sales and item reports.
- Demonstrate backup and audit-log concepts.

## Scope and limitations
This is a local academic prototype. It does not provide networked multi-user access, real payment processing, production-grade authentication, automated recipe deductions, or conflict-proof reservations.
